# Privacy Policy for PuzzleFlow

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

PuzzleFlow is a Chrome extension for tracking Chess.com puzzle training sessions.

## Information stored

PuzzleFlow stores the following information locally in your browser:

- Training session start and stop times
- Solved and failed puzzle counts
- Puzzle IDs and attempt durations
- Session statistics and personal bests
- User settings
- Optional session and milestone screenshots

This information is stored using Chrome Storage and IndexedDB. PuzzleFlow does not currently transmit this information to a remote server.

## Permissions

- `storage`: Save settings and training progress locally.
- `tabs`: Identify the active puzzle tab, communicate with the content script, and capture session screenshots.
- `activeTab`: Capture the visible puzzle tab after the user starts a training session or reaches a milestone.

PuzzleFlow only operates on Chess.com puzzle pages and does not provide engine hints, suggest moves, or play moves automatically.

## Data sharing and deletion

PuzzleFlow does not sell, share, or transfer personal information to third parties. Users can remove locally stored data by removing the extension or clearing the extension's storage through Chrome settings.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PuzzleFlow's data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
